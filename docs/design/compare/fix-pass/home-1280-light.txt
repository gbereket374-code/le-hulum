01 · THE PROBLEM
Online learning in Ethiopia is not yet made for Deaf learners.

Video lessons are now the main way people learn to code in Ethiopia. For Deaf learners, most of that teaching is out of reach.

01
No sign language. Almost no Amharic captions.

Deaf learners in Ethiopia get no sign language and almost no Amharic captions in online courses.

02
Global tools don’t speak EthSL.

Global sign tools don’t speak Ethiopian Sign Language.

03
One interpreter per lesson doesn’t scale.

Booking an interpreter for every lesson does not scale.

04 · QUESTIONS
What people ask us
How does automatic signing work?
−
We split the text into words, put them in EthSL word order and join recorded interpreter clips for each sign. Names and new terms are fingerspelled. Questions and “not” get the right face signal. If the whole sentence was recorded, we play that recording instead.
